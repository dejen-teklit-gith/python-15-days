# Warranty — Nordwind Bikes (fictional company)

All frames have a 10-year warranty against manufacturing defects.
Components such as brakes, gears and wheels are covered for 2 years.
E-bike batteries and motors are covered for 2 years or 500 charge cycles, whichever comes first.
Wear parts (tyres, chains, brake pads) and crash damage are not covered.
To make a claim, send photos and your order number to warranty@nordwind.example. We answer within 2 business days.
