# Returns & Refunds — Nordwind Bikes (fictional company)

You can return any unused product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Bikes must be unridden, with no scratches, and in the original packaging.
To start a return, open your order in the customer portal and click "Return item". You will receive a prepaid shipping label by email.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the item arrives at our warehouse.
Custom-built bikes and worn helmets cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons.
