# E-bike Battery Care — Nordwind Bikes (fictional company)

Store the battery indoors between 10 and 20 degrees Celsius; never leave it in a car in summer or below freezing.
For winter storage, keep it charged between 40 and 60 percent and top it up every two months.
A full charge takes about 4 hours with the original charger. Do not use third-party chargers, as this voids the warranty.
Range depends on assist level, rider weight and temperature: expect 60 to 120 km on the 625 Wh battery.
If the battery is dropped or shows swelling, stop using it and contact support immediately.
