# Shipping — Nordwind Bikes (fictional company)

We ship to Switzerland, Germany, Austria, France and Italy.
Standard shipping is free for orders over CHF 150; otherwise it costs CHF 9.90.
Accessories arrive in 2 to 4 business days. Complete bikes are delivered by freight in 5 to 8 business days, pre-assembled at 90 percent.
Express delivery (next business day, accessories only) costs CHF 19.
Every order gets a tracking link by email once it leaves the warehouse.
